VALUE EXTRACTION RULES (CRITICAL):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. If you see multiple totals (subtotal, freight, insurance, grand total), use the GRAND TOTAL

7. DO NOT confuse weight values (kg) with monetary values
